Lesson 1: The Basics of a Car

Welcome to your first driving lesson. In this lesson, we'll go over the basic parts of a car that you'll need to know before you start driving.

Key Components

  • Steering Wheel: Used to control the direction of the car.
  • Accelerator (Gas Pedal): Used to increase the speed of the car.
  • Brake Pedal: Used to decrease the speed of the car and to stop it.
  • Gear Selector: Used to select the gear of the car (e.g., Park, Reverse, Neutral, Drive).

Next Steps

In the next lesson, we'll cover how to start the car and how to use the gear selector.
